BEFORE THE PHOTOGRAPH

          
Preparing for
your session.

          

            Choose clothes you can sit and move in comfortably. For a family
            session, bring any small comforts your children may need. If you
            have reference photographs, describe what draws you to their light
            or mood in the enquiry notes.
          

          
After your enquiry

          

            Review the details above, then copy or download them to share with
            the studio through your preferred contact channel. A studio
            conversation can establish the session plan, available dates and
            final package details.
          

        

      

      

        
A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

        
Before we begin.

        

          What should we wear?
          

            Choose comfortable clothing and textures that photograph well
            together. Simple tones keep attention on your faces. Bring a second
            look if it fits your selected session.
          

        

        

          Can I bring a partner to a maternity session?
          

            Yes, partner-inclusive photographs are part of the maternity
            approach. Include the number of participants and any preferred
            pairings in your enquiry.
          

        

        

          Do we need to know how to pose?
          

            We can guide posture, hand position and where to look. You can also
            share any movements or poses you would prefer to avoid.
          

        

        

          How do we select photographs and prints?
          

            View photographs in the client portal and save your favorites.
            Prepare a print request by choosing an image, size, finish and
            quantity. Contact the studio to confirm your print order.
